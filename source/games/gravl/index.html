Trust the co-driver: "left four" is fast, "left one" is barely a corner off idle. Numbers run 1 (slowest) to 6 (flat). Tap to place the car, hold to rotate it, tap the opposite way to catch a slide. Cutting is allowed unless the call says don't cut. Your first completed run of the day is your blind run — it can never be replaced, so make it count.
